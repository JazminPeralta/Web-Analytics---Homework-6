## Getting started

**📌 Important Note: Installing Pillow**
Before running the game, make sure you have the Pillow library installed. This library is required to load and display the pet images and activity icons.

Open your terminal in VS Code and run:

python -m pip install Pillow

**Python Environment**
This game uses the **wa (Python 3.13.7)** environment.

## Introduction
Jazmin's Pet Daycare is pet care game where you can adopt your own pet and take care of its needs by keeping your pet happy, healthy, and well-rested. Earn coins by completing activities and work toward unlocking new pets!

*please note that the pet 'cat' cannot be unlocked at this stage*



## How To Play
**Choose Your Pet:** Start by selecting the free dog and giving it a name. Other pets are displayed as locked and require coins to unlock.

**Take Care of Your Pet:** Your pet's hunger, energy, and happiness will decrease over time. Pay attention to its expressions and messages to figure out what it needs.

**Complete Activities:** Feed your pet when it's hungry, let it sleep when it's tired, and play with it when it's sad.

**Earn Coins:** Every time you successfully complete an activity, you earn 1 coin!

**Collect Coins:** Keep taking care of your pet to increase your coin balance and work toward reaching the prices displayed for other pets.

## Game Controls
**Mouse Click:** Select your pet and interact with buttons.

**Click and Drag:** Hold down the left mouse button on an activity icon.

**Drop:** Release the icon over your pet to complete the activity.

**Main Menu:** Return to the pet selection screen without losing your collected coins.
